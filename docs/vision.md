# Paulitakes — Vision & Goals

_Last updated: August 26, 2026_

The "why" behind the site. `product-doc.md` says what it does; `technical-design.md` says how; this says what it's for and how to judge whether it's working.

## What this is

Paulitakes (paulitakes.com) is my personal sports blog: opinion and analysis in a first-person fan voice, with YouTube videos linked alongside written takes. It's a custom-built platform (Next.js App Router, Drizzle, Neon, Better Auth, TanStack Query) that took about two weeks to build.

## Why a custom platform

This was a deliberate choice, not an oversight:

- I wanted to build something, and I enjoyed building it. The build itself was part of the point.
- It cost little effort to create and maintenance is minimal.
- I own it outright — the domain, the content, the stack. It can eventually connect to the broader sports platform work (Picks Leagues and beyond).
- Reach is a secondary goal. If an audience happens, it happens by accident. I'm not optimizing for distribution, so Substack/Ghost/Medium solve a problem I don't have.

**Decision: keep the custom platform.** The platform question is settled — the open question is the writing.

## The real goal: ship posts

The failure mode to avoid: the blog becomes the project and the writing never ships. The site's success isn't measured by the stack — it's measured by whether posts exist.

Implication for engineering work: platform tasks earn their place by removing friction from writing and publishing (or by fixing something a reader would notice). A backlog item that doesn't do one of those is optional.

## Content philosophy

**Quality ≠ length.** Quality for opinion writing means timeliness plus a real point of view, not comprehensiveness. A tight take with one sharp argument beats a sprawling essay that hedges. Long drafts sit in the queue and go stale.

Two content tracks:

1. **Reactive takes (the default).** One take, under ~600 words, shipped within 48 hours of the thing happening. The constraint protects quality by forcing me to find the one argument worth making.
2. **Reflective long-form (occasional).** Less reactionary, more evergreen pieces when a topic deserves depth. These are the exception, not the standard unit of content.
